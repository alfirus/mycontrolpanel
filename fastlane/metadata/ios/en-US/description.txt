MyControlPanel is a pitch-black IT Control Center for your servers. Connect over SSH and see live metrics, cronjobs, apps, alerts, and logs refresh into one calm dashboard, with a built-in terminal and an AI assistant that knows your fleet (bring your own provider key or run Ollama locally).

Live today: server metrics via a one-click stdlib-only agent, cron/app/alert discovery over SSH, real log tail, terminal, and live GitLab issues and MRs. UI-ready with wiring on the roadmap: GitHub, CI/CD timeline, Security findings — each labelled DEMO in-app until wired. Background alerting is an explicit opt-in (default OFF, ~15 min cadence, on-device only, no push relay). Credentials stay in the OS keychain; the app is MIT-licensed, local-first, with no account and no tracking.
